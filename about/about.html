👴 About Me

My passion for game development goes back to childhood. I used to create games on paper to play with my family before I had access to a computer. I’ve always loved logical game mechanic, astonishing role-playing and detailed universe like Dungeons & Dragons and Warhammer.

🔨 Current Project

I'm building an untitled 2D isometric action RPG inspired by Diablo and Path of Exile. Development started in March 2025.

My goal is to release the game on various platforms like Steam, Kongregate, Itch.io, GameJolt, Epic and GOG. I intend to continue iterating and expanding the world and story updates after updates, sequels after sequels.

I'm all in as a solo game dev which if i succeed aspire to build a small studio inspired by creative studios like Ankama (Dofus, Wakfu) and Motion Twin (Hordes, Mush)
