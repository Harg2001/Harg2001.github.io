🔨 Current Project

I'm building an untitled 2D isometric action RPG inspired by Diablo and Path of Exile in terms of universe, by Hades and Curse of the Dead Gods in terms of technique. Development started in March 2025.

My goal is to release the game on various platforms like Steam, Kongregate, Itch.io, GameJolt, Epic and GOG. I intend to continue iterating and expanding the world and story updates after updates, sequels after sequels.

I'm all in as a solo game dev which if i succeed aspire to build a small studio inspired by creative studios like Ankama (Dofus, Wakfu) and Motion Twin (Hordes, Mush).
