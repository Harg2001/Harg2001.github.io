👴 About my past

I was born in 1986, the year the Chernobyl nuclear power plant melted down, and the same year Halley's Comet came by to say hello.

My main passion has always been games (and videogames), I made my first one on paper to play with my family before I even had access to a computer (a mix of "Gamebook in which YOU are the hero!" and hand-drawn maze). Despite being very good at math at school, i couldn’t attend a Video Game High School (it was too far and too expensive at the time) and fallback completing an IT bachelor/master crossed with one year of graphic design.


Then i looked up for a job in video games industry with close to zero chance of success. I tried self-employment for a year and managed to create a few prototypes that eventually went online, but without marketing and with expectations that didn’t match reality, it didn’t lead anywhere. So I drifted to some IT corporates, becoming a tiny human gear that helped make my bosses richer, like so many others.






At some point, i discovered achievement hunting in games with some friends. I tried so hard and got so far and now i'm having difficulties to let it go.

Later, i met my wife and was slightly distracted from this universe, my daughter came and i was much more distracted than never.

Close to 40 year old, i suddenly felt i had dropped my life somewhere. I realized the last 10 years had gone by with no meaningful memories connected to my passion. I was freed from the IT corporates and decided to return to indie game development against all advice. I'm all in now and i'm no longer accepting any more failure.

Other interests

Besides games, i have a strong interest in fantasy, mythology, science fiction, anime and electronic music.

I enjoy discussing scientific and technical topics (my first academic paper was on fractals). I’m also interested about start-ups, innovative small businesses, and crowdfunding projects (I wrote a second thesis on that subject).

Back to games, I especially love innovative game mechanic, unusual character designs, and structured universes similar to Dungeons & Dragons or Warhammer. I'm addicted to my passion, even thinking about it in my sleep and i talk about that all the time.

Today, I live in Belgium at a stone’s throw from "Brasse-Temps", a place where real trolls live in complete safety.
