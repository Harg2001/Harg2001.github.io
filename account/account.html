When you opt out, you stop receiving promotional, marketing or update emails. Transactional email (like an order confirmation) cannot be opted out. You improve your digital footprint and privacy, but you might miss out on product launches, discounts, or important service changes. This website shows transparency and care about user trust. We prefer to reduces spam complaints and improves sender reputation, which helps future emails reach the engaged users. We use this also as a feedback opportunity, high opt-out rates can signal poor targeting or irrelevant content, prompting strategy improvements.


When you delete your account, You can no longer log in or use any services tied to that account. All personalized settings, saved content, and history are erased. Except certain information for legal or operational reasons (e.g. billing records or security logs) Active subscriptions are canceled. You won’t be charged going forward. You'll reduce your digital footprint and help prevent identity theft. There is no grace period, the deletion will be immediate and non-reversable. Always back up important data before pulling the plug.


When you log out, your active session ends, meaning you must re-authenticate to access protected areas again. The authentication token stored in a cookie is deleted. This prevents the browser from sending valid credentials with future requests. Logging out helps prevent session hijacking—especially on shared or public devices. It ensures that even if someone accesses the browser afterward, they can't impersonate the user. A logout also invalidates the session on the server. This prevent re-use of stolen tokens.
